Four Cuts, Four Antennas#

The same four pattern cuts, E-plane and H-plane in co-pol and cross-pol, on a dipole, a small loop, a patch, and a horn. The antenna view shows what each cut means; the chamber view shows how a turntable and a source horn take it. Lesson 9 uses the dipole; this is the full tool.
