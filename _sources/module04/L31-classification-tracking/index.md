# L31 - Classification and Tracking

## Learning Objectives

<ol class="lo-list" style="--module: '4'; counter-reset: lo 7;">
  <li>I can integrate FMCW radar processing with a phased-array front-end to track a moving target.</li>

---

What the radar does with a detection once it has one: classifying a
target from its micro-Doppler signature (the spectrogram and a few features
of it), and tracking it in range with an alpha-beta filter on the detections
and in angle with monopulse, recalled from the L24 Lab.
