Materials#

Everything you download or reference in this course lives on this page: textbooks, the equation sheet and charts, every practice set, and every lab sheet. Each lesson page links its own materials in place; this is the one place they are all together.

Textbooks & References#

We draw from two primary references throughout the course, both provided here as PDFs and referenced by chapter/section in each lesson’s “For next time” reading list. A third standard text is listed as an alternate.

  • Milligan — Modern Antenna Design (Thomas A. Milligan, IEEE Press/Wiley) Deep treatment of antenna theory and design. Primary textbook for Modules 1–3.

  • Rohde & Schwarz — Antenna Basics (Whitepaper 8GE01) Compact, well-illustrated primer on antenna properties, measurement, and terminology. Excellent complement to Milligan for the foundations.

  • Balanis — Antenna Theory: Analysis and Design (Constantine A. Balanis, 4th ed., Wiley) The standard graduate-level reference, and the one most antenna literature cites. Not provided here — the lesson reading lists offer it as an alternate to Milligan wherever the chapters cover the same ground.

Equation Sheet & Charts#

  • ECE 444 Equation Sheet (PDF) — one landscape page, front and back, organized by module. Modules 4–5 are added as those lessons are authored.

  • Blank Smith chart (PDF, print) — the chart bound into the L4 lab packet.

  • Impedance–admittance Smith chart (PDF, color) — red impedance grid over blue admittance grid, for designs that cross from \(Z\) to \(Y\).

  • Blank Smith chart (SVG) — vector, for zooming on screen.

  • Smith chart walkthrough (PDF, 30 pages) — the chart from the ground up: the normalized \(Z_n = R/Z_0 \pm jX/Z_0\), the constant-\(R\) and constant-\(X\) circles, constant-VSWR circles, and matching worked on the chart with stubs, quarter-wave sections and lumped L–C. Good company for Lesson 4 and the Lesson 13 measurement lab. By Frank A. Lynch (W4FAL), 24 April 2008; reproduced with attribution, original at smithchart.org.

Hardware & Software#

  • ADALM-PHASER (Analog Devices) — phased-array platform used in Modules 3–4 for beamforming and FMCW radar labs

  • RTL-SDR / HackRF — introductory software-defined radio for Lesson 1 demos

  • Antenna simulation software — 4nec2 (NEC-2), used in the L8 dipole simulation lab

  • Vector network analyzer (VNA) — used for impedance / S-parameter measurement labs

  • Anechoic chamber — used for pattern measurement labs
