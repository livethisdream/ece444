## Learning Objectives

<ol class="lo-list" style="--module: '5';">
  <li>I can choose and justify array steering and taper weights to optimize detection performance for a specified scenario.</li>
  <li>I can integrate FMCW radar processing with a phased-array front-end to track a moving target.</li>
  <li>I can classify a detected target from its micro-Doppler signature and report the classifier's accuracy.</li>
  <li>I can present system performance results and defend engineering trade-offs in a technical briefing.</li>
</ol>

---

Detect, classify, and track a moving target with the ADALM-PHASER, then brief
the results. The project runs in two phases after the kickoff, on the calendar
rather than as numbered lessons.

## Kickoff

Capstone scenario, teams, and plan: detect, classify, and track a moving
target. Objective 5.1.

## Phase 1 — Array Optimization

Choose steering and taper weights and evaluate detection performance.
Objective 5.1.

## Phase 2 — Radar Integration and Demonstration

Integrate FMCW detection, micro-Doppler classification, and tracking, overlay
tracks on the array pattern, and brief the results. Objectives 5.2, 5.3, 5.4.
